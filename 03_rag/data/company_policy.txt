Acme Corp Leave Policy

Annual Leave:
Every employee gets 18 days of annual leave per year.
Leave must be applied 7 days in advance.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to 5 days only.

Sick Leave:
Employees get 10 sick leaves per year.
Medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive sick days.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees can work from home 2 days per week.
WFH must be approved by the reporting manager.
WFH is not allowed on Mondays and Fridays.
